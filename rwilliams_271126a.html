Coach's Fundamentals (Hover or tap tips to expand)

1. Ready Position

  • ● Paddle Up & Out Front +
    Keep your paddle at chest height and visible in your peripheral vision to cut down reaction time at the net.
  • ● Athletic Stance & Loose Grip +
    Stay on the balls of your feet with knees slightly bent and hold the paddle at a relaxed 4/10 grip pressure.

2. Movement

  • ● Split-Step on Contact +
    Perform a light hop right as your opponent strikes the ball so you can push off explosively in any direction.
  • ● Move on a String +
    Shift laterally together with your partner as a unit to cover angles and close down open gaps down the middle.

3. Serving

  • ● Aim Deep in the Box +
    Push your serve deep toward the baseline to pin your opponents back and make their third shot harder.
  • ● Smooth Low-to-High Swing +
    Use a consistent pendulum motion and stay behind the baseline until the third shot bounces.

4. Drop Shots

  • ● Lift from the Shoulder +
    Keep your wrist firm and guide the ball upward so it peaks on your side and lands softly in the kitchen.
  • ● Follow the Shot Forward +
    Move up to the non-volley zone line behind a good drop shot—don't admire it from the baseline.

5. Speed Ups

  • ● Attack Above Net Height +
    Be patient during dink rallies; speeding up a ball below the net forces you to hit upward and leaves you vulnerable.
  • ● Target Dominant Hip/Shoulder +
    Aim at your opponent's body to jam their swing instead of aiming for risky outer sidelines.

6. Volleys

  • ● Compact Punch, No Backswing +
    Keep your swing short and block the ball out in front of your body using your opponent's pace.
  • ● Soften Hands for Resets +
    Relax your grip on hard drives to absorb the pace and drop the ball safely back into the kitchen.

Summary

Great pickleball comes down to patience and position: build the point from a balanced ready stance, earn your way to the kitchen line with smart movement and soft drop shots, and wait for the high ball before pulling the trigger.
